Abertis / Cellnex · Hispasat 30.0°W

Generate the complete DX suite from your browser

Web port of scripts/Abertis_DX_Suite.py. It reads the channel database and produces identical Enigma2 lamedb feed services, the astra.conf relay config, the IPTV bouquet and an optional SoftCam.Key. Everything runs locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded or written to your receiver.

Preserves every other entry. Generated transponders and services whose name contains “abertis” are replaced; a .bak is written on the receiver.

Also removes transponders matching the generated ones and stale “abertis” services, then adds the fresh entries (script --edit-lamedb).
